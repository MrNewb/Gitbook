# MrNewbBeeKeeping

Get this today at [https://mrnewbscripts.tebex.io/](https://mrnewbscripts.tebex.io/)

MrNewbBeeKeeping is a beekeeping gameplay script for FiveM servers built around hive management, honey production, item progression, and roleplay-friendly farming loops.

It is designed to give players a clear resource chain to work through, while still being flexible enough for servers that want to expand, rebalance, or theme the system around their own economy.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/BEEKEEPING_THUMB.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Overview

At its core, MrNewbBeeKeeping gives players a full beekeeping loop centered around building and maintaining hives, stocking them with bees, and collecting the products they produce.

## What The Script Is Good For

* adding a farming or production job with a clear item loop
* giving players a slower paced gathering and crafting activity
* building out rural, civilian, or economy-focused roleplay
* creating additional sellable or craftable resources for your server economy
* supporting long-term progression with hive-related item setup and administration

If you need help, it is best to include your framework, inventory, and a short description of what is not working so the issue can be narrowed down quickly.
