# MrNewbPlateCarriers

Get this today at [https://mrnewbscripts.tebex.io/](https://mrnewbscripts.tebex.io/package/6630425)

MrNewbPlateCarriers is a tactical armor script for FiveM servers built around wearable plate carriers, repairable armor systems, and clean server-side control over whether a player is geared up or not.

It is designed for servers that want armor to feel like real equipment instead of a flat stat toggle, while still staying manageable for administration and other scripts.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/NEWBTEMPLATE.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Overview

At its core, MrNewbPlateCarriers gives players physical carrier items they can wear, track, and remove through normal gameplay or through server-side logic.

## What The Script Is Good For

* turning armor into an actual item-driven system instead of a simple armor refill
* giving police, security, military, or criminal factions themed tactical gear
* supporting higher-end progression with stronger carriers and stronger repair plates
* letting other resources check or remove a player vest through exports
* building more grounded inventory-based combat preparation into your server

If you need help, include your framework, inventory, and a short description of what part of the vest or repair flow is not working. That usually makes the issue much faster to diagnose.
